Drawmaha Duel

Heads-up. Nine cards, two flops, one draw. Your final five play as a 5-card hand and as a PLO5 hand on each board — three contests, and sweeping all three scoops for double.

Each of the 3 contests pays this amount; a scoop pays double (6×).

Play heads-up against a bot that solves every flop for an approximate equilibrium before it draws. Afterwards (or mid-hand, if you peek) the study panel shows the EV of every keep for your hand, the equilibrium mix, and how the in-position player should react to each draw count.

How Drawmaha Duel works
  • Heads-up. Each player is dealt 9 cards, and two 3-card flops (Board A and Board B) are dealt face up.
  • The player out of position acts first: keep 2–5 cards and discard the rest (4–7). Discard exactly 4 and you keep 5 and draw nothing — that's patting. Discard more and you draw back up to 5.
  • How many cards OOP draws is announced. Only then does the player in position choose their own keep.
  • Discards are dead. After both draws, each board gets a turn and a river.
  • Your 5 cards are scored three ways, each worth one stake: as a plain 5-card hand, and as a PLO5 hand on each board (exactly 2 of your cards + exactly 3 board cards).
  • Win all three outright and you scoop: double money (6 stakes instead of 3). An exact tie in a contest is a push — and means no scoop.
  • The button switches every hand.
